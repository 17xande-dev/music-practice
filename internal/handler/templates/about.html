About Music Practice

A place to practise scales and pieces on a digital piano, keyboard or electric guitar, with your playing shown and graded as you go. It runs entirely in your browser.

Privacy

  • Your playing stays on your device. Notes from your instrument are graded in the browser and never sent anywhere.
  • Your history and scores stay in this browser. Practice history is kept in local storage, and scores you add are kept in the browser's IndexedDB. Nothing is uploaded. Clearing site data, or the button on the Progress page, removes it; the Progress page can also export it.
  • No accounts, no cookies, no ads. Visits are counted with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and doesn't track you across sites.

Open source

The code is on GitHub under the MIT licence. Issues and ideas are welcome there.
